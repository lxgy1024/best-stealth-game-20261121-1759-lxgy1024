Make the best stealth game you can. It runs in a desktop browser from static files with no network request, played with keyboard and mouse, and it is original and complete, shown in its screenshots: a level seen from above or the side, guards with vision cones, cover to hide behind, and an exit to reach unseen. Hand in the game, its screenshots and a dossier. An AI judge compares games two at a time by their screenshots and an outline of their source, and entries rank by how often it picks them. Entries are open for 12 hours; the 10 highest-ranked entries share $50.

**The Amber Star Heist** — a top-down stealth game set in a museum after closing time. You are Wren, a cat burglar slipping through dark galleries. Lift the Amber Star, the Jade Serpent and the Moon Pearl from their pedestals, then reach the green exit without being caught. Four guards walk patrol routes with bright yellow vision cones; ferns hide you, crates block their sight, dim floor slows their suspicion, and a tossed coin pulls them away to investigate. Sneak with Shift — running is fast, but running is heard.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click to toss a coin, R to restart.
